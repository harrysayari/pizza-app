# Voice and copy

Write like a calm friend standing next to you at the counter. Short, direct, specific.

## Rules

- **Imperative first.** Start steps with a verb: `Add the salt water slowly.` Not `You should add...`.
- **One idea per sentence.** Two sentences per step at most. Put the reason after the instruction only when it changes what the cook does.
- **Name the action on buttons.** `Make my plan`, `Done, next step`, `Add to calendar`, `Use Sunday 6:00 pm`. Never `Submit`, `OK` or `Continue`.
- **Amounts.** Number, space, `g`: `202 g`. Round to whole grams. Salt, yeast and honey are shown the same way.
- **Times.** `7:25 pm`, `Fri`, `Sat`. Durations: `Wait 1 hr`, `Wait 18 hr`, `15 min`.
- **Problems.** Say what is true and offer the next move. `The earliest it can be ready is Sunday 6:00 pm.` Never `Error`, never an apology.
- **Voice input.** Show what was heard as tags and let the cook fix it with a tap.
- **No** emoji, exclamation marks, jokes, or cooking cliches in instructions.

## Examples

| Situation | Copy |
|---|---|
| Plan screen title | `Plan your pizza night` |
| Size chip | `14"` with `310 g ball` |
| Too soon | `Not enough time for Saturday 7:30 pm` / `This dough needs about 22 hours. The earliest it can be ready is Sunday 6:00 pm.` |
| Step title | `Add the salt water slowly` |
| Step text | `Pour the salt water into the bowl in three parts, not all at once. Mix slowly after each part.` |
| Wait | `Rest uncovered, 15 min` |
| Timer finished | `Time is up` |
| Calendar | `Added to your calendar` / `You will get a reminder for every step.` |
| Voice not understood | `Try: 4 pizzas, 14 inch, Saturday dinner` |
